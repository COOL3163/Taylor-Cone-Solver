\section{Safety, Ethics, and Compliance}

This work presents a computational model with no physical experiments conducted by the authors. The solver operates on standard desktop computers with no special safety considerations.

Validation against published experimental images uses properly cited literature data. No human subjects, animal subjects, or controlled substances were involved in this research.

The computational apparatus requires no high-voltage equipment, hazardous materials, or specialized laboratory facilities. All numerical simulations were performed on standard personal computers.
